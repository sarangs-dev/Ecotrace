# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
Personal Carbon Footprint Calculator[cite: 1]

## Problem
People understand that everyday lifestyle choices contribute to environmental impact, but they lack visibility into the approximate magnitude of their emissions, which activities contribute the most, and what practical changes they can adopt to reduce them[cite: 1].

## Target Users
* Primary: Individuals seeking to understand and lower their personal carbon footprint without needing specialized environmental science knowledge[cite: 1].
* Future: Students, households, and environment-conscious groups tracking lifestyle adjustments over time[cite: 1].

## Goal
Help individuals estimate their approximate carbon footprint across key everyday activities, identify their primary emission drivers, and obtain actionable, AI-driven reduction recommendations[cite: 1].

## Core Features
1. **Interactive CLI / Input Interface:** Simple prompts to collect activity data across core lifestyle categories[cite: 1].
2. **Deterministic Calculation Engine:** Transparent, rule-based formula system (`Activity Data × Emission Factor`) storing emission constants independently from business logic[cite: 1].
3. **Category Breakdown & Summary Display:** Clear presentation of category-wise emissions alongside the total estimated monthly carbon footprint[cite: 1].
4. **Input Validation:** Guardrails for handling invalid inputs, negative numbers, missing values, and incorrect menu selections[cite: 1].
5. **AI Interpretation & Recommendation Engine:** LLM-assisted analysis that reviews calculated totals and provides tailored, practical reduction tips without hallucinating numerical estimates[cite: 1].

## MVP
* **Lifestyle Inputs:** Collect data for Transportation, Electricity, and Food[cite: 1].
* **Calculation Engine:** Python logic mapping user inputs to predefined emission factors[cite: 1].
* **Results Output:** Display category-wise footprint (kg CO₂e) and total monthly estimated footprint[cite: 1].
* **Validation:** Robust handling of empty inputs, non-numeric values, and out-of-range options[cite: 1].
* **AI Integration:** Post-calculation prompt delivering personalized reduction suggestions based strictly on calculated category drivers[cite: 1].

## Out of Scope
* User accounts, authentication, profiles, and cloud synchronization[cite: 1]
* Web UI, mobile applications, or frontend frameworks[cite: 1]
* Real-time GPS, IoT device, or smart-home data ingestion[cite: 1]
* Carbon offset purchasing, leaderboards, social feeds, or gamification[cite: 1]
* Complex microservices, Docker/Kubernetes deployment, or relational database setups[cite: 1]
* Chatbots, autonomous conversational agents, or predictive machine learning models[cite: 1]

## Success Criteria
A user should be able to:
1. Launch the local Python application without runtime errors[cite: 1].
2. Enter activity values for transportation, electricity, and food[cite: 1].
3. Receive clear error messages and re-prompts if invalid data is provided[cite: 1].
4. View an itemized breakdown of emissions for each category in kg CO₂e[cite: 1].
5. View an overall estimated monthly carbon footprint explicitly labeled as an estimate[cite: 1].
6. Receive practical, context-specific reduction tips generated by AI based on their highest-emitting categories[cite: 1].
